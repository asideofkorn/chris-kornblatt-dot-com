About the notebook

This is a place for developing ideas rather than performing certainty. Some notes begin with product work; others with a mountain route, a piece of ceramic, a business, or an unfamiliar technology.

The common subject is how we understand systems well enough to act within them.

Nothing is published here yet. What follows is the working outline—the notes I’m drafting, listed so the direction is visible rather than implied. Until the first one lands, the case studies are the better read.
