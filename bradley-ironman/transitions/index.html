Transitions

How long the field spent getting from swim to bike (T1) and bike to run (T2). T1 here includes the climb out of the river and the run to transition, so it's longer than at most races.

Table view

Reading the data

  • T1 is the time between the swim exit mat and the bike start mat; T2 is between the bike finish mat and the run start mat. Athletes missing either mat are left out of that transition.
  • The fastest and slowest 1% are clipped from the histograms so the shape is readable; they still count in the table.
  • Source: official timing-mat crossings (RTRT.me). Athletes are anonymous; only Bradley is identified.
