Reading the data

  • Source: official timing-mat crossings from RTRT.me, the service behind the IRONMAN Tracker app. Athletes are anonymous; only Bradley is identified.
  • The vertical scale is stretched per sport so the swim and transitions are visible. Within each sport, distance is to scale.
  • "Didn't finish" means no finish time. It uses the last mat each athlete crossed, since chips sometimes miss a mat. Disqualifications after finishing aren't in the timing data.
  • "Didn't start" counts registered athletes with no time at any mat, so it includes anyone who withdrew before race day.
  • Red markers are 2026 cutoffs: bike mile 56 by 3:00 PM, run checkpoints at Tower Bridge by 9:45 PM and 10:45 PM, and course close at midnight.
