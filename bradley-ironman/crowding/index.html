Reading the data

  • Athletes are counted from their start until their last timing mat, so anyone between mats when the data was pulled isn't counted past their last mat.
  • The bike is two out-and-back laps and the run is two laps, so a stretch of road appears more than once down the chart.
  • Source: official timing-mat crossings (RTRT.me). Athletes are anonymous; only Bradley is identified.
