Reading the data

  • Place at a mat is by race time from each athlete's own start (the swim was a rolling start), out of everyone who started; athletes who haven't reached that mat yet, or dropped out before it, count as behind him.
  • Before everyone has passed a mat, places are "so far": later starters with faster race times can still move ahead. Final places come after the course closes.
  • Mats where Bradley's chip wasn't read are skipped.
  • Source: official timing-mat crossings (RTRT.me). Other athletes are anonymous.
