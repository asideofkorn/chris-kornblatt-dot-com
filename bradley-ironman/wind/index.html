IRONMAN California · Sacramento · Oct 18, 2026

Wind on the bike

The bike is two out-and-back laps on the same Delta roads, so riders cover the same 7.3 miles going south and coming back north. Comparing their speeds in each direction shows the wind, and the weather record shows what it actually was.

Reading the data

  • Rider speeds come from official timing-mat crossings (RTRT.me): the distance between two mats divided by the time between them. Athletes are anonymous; only Bradley is identified.
  • Speed differences aren't only wind: later in a lap riders are more tired, and faster riders reach each stretch earlier. Comparing directions at the same time of day reduces this but doesn't remove it.
  • Weather: observed wind from the National Weather Service station at Sacramento Executive Airport (KSAC), about 6 miles north of the course; modeled hourly wind from Open-Meteo at the course's north and south ends.
