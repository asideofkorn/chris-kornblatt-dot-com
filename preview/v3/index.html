Making AI extraction trustworthy.

The problem

Shipping information arrives across documents and systems. Extraction alone isn't enough: people need to know where information came from and how to correct it.

The approach

Combine document intake, structured extraction, source provenance, and review into a workflow designed for trust. This is an early case-study outline, not a final account of outcomes.
