Flood Watch

Are the conditions that produce catastrophic California flooding beginning to develop? A public research project connecting storms, watersheds, and infrastructure.

Early prototype — not a live warning system. Reservoir updates are being connected; other measurements and forecasts are not yet connected. For immediate safety information, use official National Weather Service alerts.

What we're watching

Reservoir inflows and releases, soil moisture, Sierra snowpack and freezing levels, river forecasts, and clusters of atmospheric rivers. The goal is to explain how these conditions interact—not to invent a single megaflood probability.

Why this exists

Reading about California's Great Flood of 1861–1862, and driving through the Sacramento–San Joaquin Delta alongside its levees, prompted a question: how early could we recognize a dangerous sequence of storms? This project begins by making existing evidence easier to interpret.

Independent research prototype. Not affiliated with NOAA or California DWR. Never substitute this page for official flood warnings.
